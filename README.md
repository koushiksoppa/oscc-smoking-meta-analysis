# Association Between Tobacco Smoking and Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

## Overview

This repository documents a systematic review and meta-analysis investigating the association between tobacco smoking and the risk of Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC).

The project follows internationally accepted systematic review guidelines, including the PRISMA 2020 framework, and aims to provide a reproducible research workflow from literature search to statistical meta-analysis.

---

## Research Question

**Is tobacco smoking associated with an increased risk of Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC)?**

---

## Objectives

### Primary Objective

- Evaluate the association between tobacco smoking and Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma.

### Secondary Objectives

- Compare findings across different populations.
- Assess study quality.
- Estimate the pooled effect size using meta-analysis.
- Evaluate heterogeneity and publication bias.

---

## Study Design

- Systematic Review
- Meta-analysis
- Observational Studies (Case-Control and Cohort Studies)
