| **A completed systematic review** | A review in which a human reviewer has screened records, retrieved full texts, judged eligibility, extracted data with source locations, appraised risk of bias, and synthesised the result. **This project has not done any of that.** |

## Honest summary

The project is a **rigorous, reproducible systematic review completed through the search and
deduplication stages**, with tested infrastructure ready for every remaining stage and a
statistical module whose validation status is documented including what it does **not** establish.

It is **not** a completed systematic review or meta-analysis, and no artefact in the repository
claims otherwise: `results/validation/prisma_counts.md` carries a banner stating that 0 of 194
records are screened and that the zeros below it mean *work not done*, not *nothing eligible*.
